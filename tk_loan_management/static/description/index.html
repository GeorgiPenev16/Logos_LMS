Customer Loan Management Features

Advanced Dashboard

The Dashboard shows static data, loans statistics, charges statistics and charts of best-selling loan, loan distribution and recovery statistics, disbursed loan amount by months, received charge.

EMI & Interest Calculation

The manager calculates the EMI and interest amount for the loan, ensuring accurate repayment details, providing clarity to the customer, and facilitating a smooth loan repayment process.

NOC Document

The NOC document outlines loan details, confirms zero dues, and states the company has no objection to the loan.

Signature Certificate

The signature certificate shows the customer’s signature, the date they signed the sanction letter, and loan details at that time.

Collateral Property & Security Management

The manager can add the customer's collateral property as security for the loan, ensuring proper loan backing.

Post Closure Management

After loan closure, both the customer and manager can download the closure report letter and NOC document for the loan.

Risk Analysis

The manager can analyze the loan's risk based on the customer's documents and collateral property provided.

Approval Workflow

First, the department manager approves the documents and the requested loan before proceeding with further steps in the process.

Disbursement Management

The loan is disbursed once the customer signs the sanction letter and pays the processing fee.

Terms & Conditions

Different terms and conditions can be set for each loan type based on specific requirements and policies.

Website

On the website's loan request page, customers can apply for a loan, explore various loan types, and review the available loan terms to choose the best option for their needs.

KYC & Document Verifications

The department managers can verify the customer's KYC and documents, ensuring all information is accurate and complete, allowing the loan process to proceed smoothly.

Portal Loan Management

Customers can view his loan details, sign sanction letter and download sanction letter, signature certificate, closure letter, pre-closure letter, and NOC.

Sanction Letter

The sanction letter provides details of the sanctioned loan, including installment breakdown, terms & conditions, and repayment terms.

Closure Report Letter

The closure letter provides loan details, installment breakdown, penalty details, and confirms that the loan has been fully repaid.

Document Management

The manager can approve, reject, or resubmit the customer's documents based on the review and requirements.

Pre Sanction of Loan

The loan is pre-sanctioned by the department manager, after which the customer signs the sanctioned loan agreement.

User Roles Management

One role exists: the department manager approves/rejects loans, verifies documents, and sends the sanction letter for signature.

PDF Reports

The manager and customer can download PDFs of the sanction letter, signature certificate, NOC, and closure letter.

Penalty Management

Penalty is applied if the customer misses an installment and if percentage-based, compound interest is charged on unpaid penalties.

Repayment Handling

The installment invoice is generated based on the loan term—monthly, quarterly, or yearly—to ensure timely repayment.

Leads

In the leads section, loans requested by customers are displayed and converted into opportunities. Once an opportunity is confirmed, the customer’s loan is created from the lead.
